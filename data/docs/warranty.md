# Warranty

## What is covered
Backpacks and tents carry a 2 year warranty against manufacturing defects such as broken zips, failed seams and delaminating fabric.

## What is not covered
Normal wear and tear, damage from accidents, and items repaired by a third party are not covered.

## Making a claim
Email support with your order number and photos of the defect. If the claim is approved we repair the item or send a replacement free of charge.
