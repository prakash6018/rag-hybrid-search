# Account and security

## Resetting your password
Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page. A reset link valid for 30 minutes is sent to your email address.

## Two-factor authentication
Turn on two-factor authentication in Settings, then Security. You can use an authenticator app or SMS codes.

## Deleting your account
Account deletion is permanent. Request it from Settings, then Privacy. Your order history is removed after 30 days.
