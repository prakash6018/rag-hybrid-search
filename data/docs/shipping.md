# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days within the country. Express delivery arrives in 1 to 2 business days and costs 9.99. Orders placed before 2 pm ship the same day.

## International shipping
We ship to 40 countries. International orders take 7 to 14 business days. Customs duties and import taxes are paid by the customer on delivery.

## Tracking an order
A tracking link is emailed as soon as the parcel leaves the warehouse. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan.
